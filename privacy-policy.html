This privacy policy applies to the Townly app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by mert demirkiran (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Information you provide when you create an account: first name, last name, nickname, and email address. If you sign in with Google, the Application receives the name and email address associated with your Google account.
  • Your password, which is handled by Firebase Authentication. The Service Provider does not store your password in plain text.
  • Profile information you choose to add, including a profile photo.
  • Content you create in the Application, including chat messages, photos, videos, voice messages, posts, comments, and friend connections.
  • Messages you send through the in-app contact form.
  • Your device's push notification token, so the Application can deliver notifications you have allowed.
  • Online status and the time of your last activity in the Application.
  • Crash and diagnostic information, such as the operating system version and device model, collected through Firebase Crashlytics to fix errors.
  • Technical data that Google Firebase and Google Play services may process while the Application runs, which can include an Internet Protocol address (IP address).

When you choose to detect your area, the Application collects your device's location (latitude and longitude) in order to determine your district. That location and the resulting district name may be stored with your profile so the Application can show the local chat and feed for your area. The Application does not collect your location in the background and does not track your movements over time.


The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.


The Service Provider may use the information you provided to operate the Application, keep your account secure, deliver notifications, and contact you with important information and required notices about the service.


For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information, including your name, email address, nickname, profile photo, location when you detect your area, and the content you post or send. The information that the Service Provider requests will be retained and used as described in this privacy policy.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall process available on your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace. Uninstalling the Application does not delete your account. To delete your account and the data stored with it, follow the steps on the account deletion page, use the delete account option in the Application, or contact the Service Provider at the email address below.

You can decline location permission. Without it, the Application cannot detect your district automatically. You can also turn off notifications in your device settings.

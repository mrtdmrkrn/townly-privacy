Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) and CSAM Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

This policy applies to the Townly app, operated by mert demirkiran (CoolApps). Townly has a zero-tolerance policy for any content or behavior that sexually exploits, harms, or endangers children. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) are strictly prohibited.

Townly is not directed at children under 13.


1. Zero tolerance

Users may not upload, share, send, request, or solicit CSAM or any content or conduct that sexually exploits a child. This includes grooming, sexual comments directed at a minor, and any attempt to obtain sexual content from a child.

Any account that does this will be permanently removed from Townly.


2. How to report

If you see content or behavior that may exploit or endanger a child, report it immediately:

  • On a post, open the post menu and choose Report post. Select Inappropriate content or Other, and describe what you saw.
  • In the app, open Settings and choose Contact Us.
  • Email the child-safety contact at dk.mert95@gmail.com.

Please include the username, where you saw the content, and the time, if you can. Do not forward CSAM.


3. What we do with a report

Reports are reviewed by Townly. When a report involves suspected CSAM or CSAE, Townly will:

  • remove the content and permanently ban the account;
  • report the incident to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant law-enforcement authority;
  • preserve the information those authorities need for an investigation.
